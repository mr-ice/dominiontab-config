The Cricut has a no-cut margin -- 0,0 is not 0,0 on the mat.

The X direction the margin is .747 cm 
The Y direction the margin is .601 cm

The calculated drawing offset is to the edge of the paper.  To position
the paper edge at 0,0 we need to subract these from the calculated
offset.

The X offset is always calculated at 1.57cm.  The result after removing
.747 is 0.823cm

The Y offset varies a bit based on the height of the cards.

3.171 is the height of the taller kingdom cards (typically because there
are more than 10) and 3.942 is when they are shorter (normal).

3.171 = 2.59
3.942 = 3.341
